About LuminAqua

I built LuminAqua because I was tired of fighting with the official app for my lights. It was lackluster, and I just wanted to control my lights better.

  • Smooth day curves: real sunrises and sunsets that glide minute by minute instead of jumping between steps.
  • All my lights in one place: change one tank, or tick several and control them all at once. Feeding time, a new curve, lights off: one tap for every tank.
  • Any phone or computer: no app to install, no account, no cloud. It runs on a Raspberry Pi next to the tanks.

Free, for everyone

LuminAqua is completely free to use. I made it to solve a problem I had, and it was immediately clear it could help a lot of other people with the same lights, so I felt obliged to share it.

Make it better

If you want to improve on LuminAqua, please do. I encourage it. Fork it, change it, add the feature you've been missing, and send it back so everyone gets it. It's open source under the MIT licence.
